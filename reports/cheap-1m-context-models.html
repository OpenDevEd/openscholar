Methodology & sources

Click to expand

How candidates were selected

  1. Pulled the full OpenRouter model catalog (https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models) — 363 models on 2026-05-13.
  2. Filtered for context_length ≥ 1,000,000.
  3. Filtered for input price ≤ $1.50/M (so Gemini 2.5 Pro at $1.25/M makes the cut, but Anthropic Sonnet does not — and Sonnet doesn't hit 1M context anyway).
  4. Removed router/aggregator entries (openrouter/auto, openrouter/pareto-code, gemini-flash-latest router proxies).
  5. Cross-referenced with Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Vectara HHEM-2.3 leaderboard, NVIDIA RULER, HELMET, Princeton LongBench v2 — to the extent each model is listed.

Benchmark sources

  • Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — composite of MMLU-Pro, GPQA Diamond, Humanity's Last Exam, LiveCodeBench, SciCode, AIME, MATH-500, IFBench, AA-LCR. Updated weekly. (artificialanalysis.ai)
  • Vectara HHEM-2.3 — third-party hallucination rate on a fixed grounded-summarization eval. (github.com/vectara/hallucination-leaderboard)
  • NVIDIA RULER — multi-task long-context benchmark with retrieval, multi-hop, aggregation, and QA tasks at 4K → 128K → 1M lengths. (github.com/NVIDIA/RULER)
  • Princeton HELMET — Holistic long-context evaluation including realistic recall and reasoning tasks. (github.com/princeton-nlp/HELMET)
  • OpenAI MRCR / Graphwalks — Internal long-context benchmarks reported by OpenAI for GPT-4.1 / GPT-5 families.
  • SimpleQA / FACTS Grounding — Closed-book and grounded factuality benchmarks from OpenAI and Google DeepMind.

Why "blended 80/20"?

Academic extraction puts a large document into the prompt and asks for a small JSON object back — typically 100K–1M input tokens vs. 1K–10K output tokens. The blended 80/20 metric is conservative for output, so it slightly favors models with cheap input. Toggle the calculator above for your actual workload mix.

Caveats

  • Several models in this list are preview-stage (Gemini 3 Flash Preview, Qwen3.5 Plus 2026-02-15, Xiaomi MiMo V2.5). Pricing and capabilities can shift before GA.
  • OpenRouter prices reflect the cheapest routing tier; your provider may bill differently if you go direct.
  • "Effective context length" varies wildly across benchmarks — RULER, HELMET, and NIAH give different numbers for the same model. The numbers shown average the public RULER + HELMET results when available.
  • For models with no published HHEM/RULER score, the cell shows "—". Don't assume parity with siblings.
