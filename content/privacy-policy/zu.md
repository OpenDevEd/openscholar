# **Ulwazi esiluqoqayo, esiluyisebenzisayo, nesizathu**

Siqoqa noma sisebenzise imininingwane yomuntu elandelayo **ukuze sisebenzise i-akhawunti yakho nezici ozisebenzisayo**:

* Igama lakho, ikheli le-imeyili nesithombe sephrofayela, nezinhlangano oyilungu lazo
* Okwenzayo ku-Openscholar: ukusesha, imibuzo, amaphepha agciniwe, izilinganiso, amaqoqo, ukuskrina, ukukhishwa nezingxoxo nomsizi
* Amafayela owalayishayo, nezinhlelo ozixhumayo, njenge-Zotero
* Amakhredithi owasebenzisayo nowathengayo. I-Stripe iphatha imininingwane yekhadi, futhi sigcina kuphela uhlobo lwekhadi nezinombolo zalo ezine zokugcina

Uma usebenzisa isici se-AI, isicelo sakho nombhalo wephepha odingekayo kuthunyelwa kumhlinzeki we-AI owenza umsebenzi.

Sithumela ku-PostHog irekhodi lesicelo ngasinye se-AI nomsebenzi ngamunye wokuhlunga, ngaphandle kombhalo waso, nelokubhalisa ngakunye, inkokhelo yokuqala nokushintsha kohlelo, ukuze silandele izindleko, ukwethembeka nokukhula. Liqukethe i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho kuphela uma wamukele amakhukhi okuhlaziya.

Siqoqa noma siyasebenzisa ulwazi lomuntu siqu olulandelayo **ngokuphatha imibuzo, izikhalo noma izimangalo**:

* Amagama nemininingwane yokuxhumana
* Okuqukethwe kwezincwadi ze-imeyili: imibuzo yakho, izikhalo, impendulo yakho, nezimpendulo zethu
* Amarekhodi okuxhumana nezikhathi zawo

Siqoqa noma siyasebenzisa ulwazi olulandelayo ukuze **sihlaziye ukuthi abavakashi basebenzisa kanjani iwebhusayithi yethu, silungise izinkinga zobuchwepheshe, futhi sithuthukise izici ezingcono**:

* Ikheli le-IP (lisetshenziselwa ukuthola indawo ecishe ibe khona: izwe, idolobha, isifunda)
* Uhlobo lwesiphequluli nenguqulo yaso
* Uhlelo lokusebenza nohlobo lwedivayisi
* Ukucaca kwesikrini
* Amakhasi avakashelwe namapheya okuzulazula
* I-URL yokuthumela (iwebhusayithi owavela kuyo)
* Isikhathi osichithe emakhasini
* Ukuchofoza izinkinobho nokusebenzisana
* Ukujula kokuskrola
* Ulwazi lweseshini (i-ID yeseshini, izikhathi)
* Izihlonzi ezingabizwa ngegama lakho (i-ID yedivayisi, i-ID yeseshini)
* Okuphindwayo kweseshini (okurekhodwe ngokunyakaza kwesilo, ukuchofoza, ukuskrola, nokusebenzisana namakhasi; idatha ebucayi njengamaphasiwedi nolwazi lokukhokha ifihlwa ngokuzenzakalelayo)

**Lokho esingakuqoqi:**
* Ngaphandle kwalokho okufunayo, asiqoqi amanani okufakwa ezimweni, amaphasiwedi, noma ulwazi lokukhokha ngokuhlaziya
* Asisebenzisi ama-cookie okukhangisa
* Asidayisi idatha yakho kubakhangisi

Uma wamukela ama-cookie okuhlaziya futhi ungene ngemvume, i-PostHog ixhumanisa lolu lwazi ne-akhawunti yakho, negama lakho nekheli lakho le-imeyili, futhi ithola lokho okufunayo. I-Google Analytics nayo ithola amakhasi owavakashelayo, indlela ofike ngayo kithi, uhlobo lwedivayisi yakho nendawo yakho ecishe ibe yiyo. Ayitholi lokho okufunayo, futhi izici zayo zokukhangisa zicishiwe.

Ngaphambi kokuthi ukhethe, noma uma wenqaba ama-cookie okuhlaziya, sibala kuphela ukuvakasha, ngaphandle kwama-cookie futhi singagcini lutho edivayisini yakho ngaphandle kokukhetha kwakho: ikhasi, iwebhusayithi ovela kuyo, uhlobo lwesiphequluli sakho, nezenzo ezifana nokusesha. Asirekhodi ukuchofoza kwakho, lokho okubhalayo, indawo yakho noma i-akhawunti yakho. Ikheli lakho le-IP nohlobo lwesiphequluli kuba yikhodi eshintsha nsuku zonke, futhi asiligcini ikheli lakho le-IP.

Siqoqa noma siyasebenzisa ulwazi olulandelayo ngokuqoqa impendulo yabasebenzisi ngezinhlolovo:

* Izimpendulo zezinhlolovo
* Izimpendulo namahlaziya
* Izikhathi zokuqedwa kwezinhlolovo

**Qaphela:** Izinhlolovo zethu azicelwa amagama noma amakheli e-imeyili. Kodwa izimpendulo zezinhlolovo zingahlanganiswa nesihlonzi seseshini esingabizwa ngegama lakho ngenjongo yokuhlaziya.

# **Izisekelo ezisemthethweni namalungelo okuvikelwa kwedatha**

Ngokomthetho wokuvikelwa kwedatha we-UK, kufanele sibe nesisekelo esisemthethweni sokuqoqa nokusebenzisa ulwazi lwakho lomuntu siqu. Kukhona uhla [lwezisekelo ezisemthethweni](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/getting-started-with-gdpr/data-protection-principles-definitions-and-key-terms/#lawfulbasis) ezingaba khona ku-UK GDPR. Ungathola ulwazi oluningi ngezisekelo ezisemthethweni ewebhusayithini ye-ICO.

Isisekelo esisemthethweni esiyisebenzisayo singathinta amalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha, aveziwe ngokufushane ngezansi. Ungathola ulwazi oluningi ngamalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha nokukhululwa okungasebenza ewebhusayithini ye-ICO:

* **Ilungelo lakho lokufinyelela** – Unelungelo lokusicela izikhopi zolwazi lwakho lomuntu siqu. Ungacela ezinye izinkcukacha, njengemininingwane yokuthi sithola kuphi ulwazi lomuntu siqu nokuthi sabelana ngalo nobani. Kukhona ukukhululwa okuthile, okusho ukuthi ungaseungathole lonke ulwazi olucelayo. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokufinyelela](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#roa).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokulungisa** – Unelungelo lokusicela ukuthi silungise noma sisuse ulwazi lomuntu siqu olucabanga ukuthi alulungile noma alupheleli. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokulungisa](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rtr).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokususa** – Unelungelo lokusicela ukuthi sisuse ulwazi lwakho lomuntu siqu. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokususa](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rte).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokuvimbela ukucutshungulwa** – Unelungelo lokusicela ukuthi sinciphise indlela esingasebenzisa ngayo ulwazi lwakho lomuntu siqu. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokuvimbela ukucutshungulwa](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rtrop).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokuphikisa ukucutshungulwa** – Unelungelo lokuphikisa ukucutshungulwa kwedatha yakho yomuntu siqu. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokuphikisa ukucutshungulwa](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rto).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokudlulisa idatha** – Unelungelo lokusicela ukuthi sidlulisele ulwazi lomuntu siqu owasinika lona kwenye inhlangano, noma kuwe. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokudlulisa idatha](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rtdp).
* **Ilungelo lakho lokuhoxisa imvume** – Uma sisebenzisa imvume njengesisekelo sethu esisemthethweni, unelungelo lokuhoxisa imvume yakho noma nini. [Funda kabanzi ngelungelo lokuhoxisa imvume](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/your-data-protection-rights/#rtwc).

Uma wenza isicelo, kufanele sikuphendule ngaphandle kokulibazisa okungadingekile, futhi noma kunjalo phakathi nenyanga eyodwa.

Ukuze wenze isicelo samalungelo okuvikelwa kwedatha, sicela uxhumane nathi usebenzisa imininingwane yokuxhumana esphezulu kwalesi saziso sobumfihlo.

# **Izisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni zokuqoqa nokusebenzisa idatha yakho**

Isisekelo sethu sezomthetho sokuqoqa noma ukusebenzisa imininingwane yomuntu **ukuze sisebenzise i-akhawunti yakho nezici ozisebenzisayo** yilesi:

* **Inkontileka** – siyayidinga ukuze sinikeze insizakalo obhalisele yona. Wonke amalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha angasebenza, ngaphandle kwelungelo lokuphikisa.

* **Izithakazelo ezisemthethweni** – kulawo marekhodi ezicelo ze-AI. Siyawadinga ukuze sigcine izici ze-AI zithembekile nezindleko zazo zilawulwa.

Izisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni zokuqoqa noma zokusebenzisa ulwazi lomuntu siqu ngokuphatha imibuzo, izikhalo noma izimangalo yilezi:

* **Izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni** – siqoqa noma sisebenzisa ulwazi lwakho ngoba luzuzisa wena, inhlangano yethu noma omunye umuntu, ngaphandle kokudala ingozi engadingekile yokulimala kunoma ubani. Onke amalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha angasebenza, ngaphandle kwelungelo lokudlulisa. Izintshisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni yilezi:
  * Kumele siphendule imibuzo yabasebenzisi, izikhalo nezicelo zosekelo. Lokhu kuzuzisa abasebenzisi ngqo ngokuxazulula izinkinga zabo nokunikeza usekelo lwamakhasimende. Ukugcina amarekhodi okuxhumana kusisiza sinikeze usekelo olufanayo futhi sibheke ukuxhumana kwangaphambili.

Ukuthola ulwazi oluningi ngokusebenzisa kwethu izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni njengesisekelo esisemthethweni, ungaxhumana nathi usebenzisa imininingwane yokuxhumana ebekwe phezulu.

Izisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni zokuqoqa noma zokusebenzisa ulwazi lomuntu siqu ngokuhlaziya ukuthi abavakashi basebenzisa kanjani iwebhusayithi yethu yilezi:

* **Imvume** – sicela imvume yakho ngaphambi kokusetha ama-cookie okuhlaziya ngebhena yemvume ye-cookie. Ungahoxisa imvume noma nini ngokuthi:
  * Uchofoze "Izintandokazi zama-cookie" esiphethweni sanoma yiliphi ikhasi
  * Ususe ama-cookie ngezilungiselelo zesiphequluli sakho
  
* **Izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni** – ngedatha yokuhlaziya engadingi ama-cookie (njengamalogi eseva), sithembela ezintshisekelweni ezisemthethweni. Onke amalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha angasebenza, ngaphandle kwelungelo lokudlulisa. Izintshisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni yilezi:
  * Ukuqonda ukuthi abavakashi basebenzisa kanjani iwebhusayithi yethu kusisiza sithuthukise isipiliyoni somsebenzisi, silungise izinkinga zobuchwepheshe, sithole futhi silungise izimbungulo, futhi sithuthukise izici abasebenzisi abazidingayo ngempela. Idatha ifihlwa igama ngangokunokwenzeka, nezinzuzo zokuthuthukisa isevisi yethu zidlula umthelela omncane kakhulu emfihlweni ekuqoqeni idatha yokusebenza engabizwa ngegama.

Ukuthola ulwazi oluningi ngokusebenzisa kwethu izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni njengesisekelo esisemthethweni, ungaxhumana nathi usebenzisa imininingwane yokuxhumana ebekwe phezulu.

Izisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni zokuqoqa noma zokusebenzisa ulwazi ngokuqoqa impendulo yabasebenzisi ngezinhlolovo yilezi:

* **Izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni** – ekubaleni esikwenza ngaphandle kwama-cookie, okuchazwe ngenhla, sithembele ezithakazelweni ezisemthethweni.
* **Izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni** – siqoqa noma sisebenzisa ulwazi lwakho ngoba luzuzisa wena, inhlangano yethu noma omunye umuntu, ngaphandle kokudala ingozi engadingekile yokulimala kunoma ubani. Onke amalungelo akho okuvikelwa kwedatha angasebenza, ngaphandle kwelungelo lokudlulisa. Izintshisekelo zethu ezisemthethweni yilezi:
  * Ukuqoqa impendulo engaziwa kusisiza siqonde izidingo zabasebenzisi futhi sithuthukise iwebhusayithi yethu bonke abavakashi. Izinhlolovo azidingekile futhi azicele ulwazi oluqondile olubonisa umuntu. Izimpendulo zingahlanganiswa nesihlonzi seseshini esingabizwa ngegama ukuze sisize siqonde umongo nokuthuthukisa izinsizakalo zethu. Abasebenzisi bazuzisa ekuthuthukisweni okusekelwe kumbono oqoqiwe.

Ukuthola ulwazi oluningi ngokusebenzisa kwethu izintshisekelo ezisemthethweni njengesisekelo esisemthethweni, ungaxhumana nathi usebenzisa imininingwane yokuxhumana ebekwe phezulu.

Sisebenzisa izindlela zobuchwepheshe nezohlelo ezifanele ukuvikela ulwazi lwakho lomuntu siqu:

* **Ukubethelwa:** Idatha ibethelwa ngesikhathi isuka (HTTPS/TLS) nalapho igciniwe
* **Izilawuli zokufinyelela:** Ukufinyelela okulinganiselwe kwedatha yomuntu siqu ngokusekelwe kudingo lokwazi
* **Ukufihla igama:** Idatha yokuhlaziya ifihlwa igama ngangokunokwenzeka
* **Ukubuyekezwa kokuphepha njalo:** Siyaqapha futhi sibuyekeze izindlela zethu zokuphepha
* **Abacubunguli bedatha abaphephile:** Sisebenza kuphela nabacubunguli abahlangabezana namazinga aphezulu okuphepha
* **Ukuphatha imvume yamakhukhi:** Amakhukhi okuhlaziya nokuphindwa kwezikhathi kusebenza kuphela ngemvume yakho

# **Ama-cookie**

Sisebenzisa ama-cookie ukuqoqa ezinye zolwazi lokuhlaziya oluchazwe kulesi saziso sobumfihlo. Ulwazi oluningiliziwe ngama-cookie esiwasebenzisayo, sicela ubheke [Inqubomgomo yama-cookie](/cookie-policy).

Izinqaku ezibalulekile:
* Sisebenzisa ama-cookie okuhlaziya avela ku-PostHog (agcinwa izinsuku ezingu-365) nakuGoogle Analytics (agcinwa izinsuku ezingu-400)
* Awekho ama-cookie okukhangisa
* Ungamukela noma wenqabe ama-cookie ngebhena yethu yemvume ye-cookie
* Ungashintsha izintandokazi zakho noma nini ngokusebenzisa "Izintandokazi zama-cookie" esiphethweni
