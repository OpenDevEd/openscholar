# **Imigomo yokusetshenziswa**

Le migomo isebenza uma usebenzisa i-Openscholar, kuhlanganise newebhusayithi yayo, izici zayo ze-AI nanoma iyiphi i-Evidence Community eyiqhubayo. I-Openscholar iqhutshwa yi-Open Development & Education ("thina"). Ngokusebenzisa i-Openscholar, uyavumelana nale migomo. Uma ungavumi, sicela ungayisebenzisi.

# **I-alpha yomphakathi**

I-Openscholar ikwi-alpha yomphakathi futhi inikezelwa ukuhlola, ngakho izici, imikhawulo nezinhlelo kungashintsha noma kuphele, futhi izinto zingonakala. Gcina amakhophi akho anoma yini ongeke ukwazi ukuyilahlekelwa.

# **I-akhawunti yakho**

* Udinga i-akhawunti ezicini eziningi. Gcina imininingwane yakho yokungena iphephile, futhi usazise uma ucabanga ukuthi omunye umuntu uyisebenzisile.
* Kufanele okungenani ube neminyaka eyi-16 ukuze wenze i-akhawunti.
* Unesibopho salokho okwenzeka ngaphansi kwe-akhawunti yakho.
* Enhlanganweni, abaphathi bayo bangaphatha amalungu, amakhredithi nezilungiselelo, futhi babone ukuthi amalungu asebenzisa kanjani amakhredithi.

# **Ukusetshenziswa okwamukelekile**

Sicela ungakwenzi lokhu:

* ukwephula umthetho noma amalungelo abanye, kuhlanganise ne-copyright
* ukuzama ukugwema imikhawulo, amakhredithi, ukuphepha noma izilawuli zokufinyelela
* ukuphazamisa insizakalo, noma ukuyilayisha ngokweqile ngezicelo ezizenzakalelayo
* ukuyikhuhla noma ukuthengisa kabusha ukufinyelela ngaphandle kwemvume yethu ebhaliwe
* ukuyisebenzisa ukwakha noma ukuqeqesha insizakalo eqhudelanayo

Singakhawulela, simise noma sivale i-akhawunti ephula le migomo, noma ebeka insizakalo noma abanye abantu engozini.

# **Okuqukethwe kwakho**

Okulayishayo noma okubhalayo kuhlala kungokwakho. Lokho kuhlanganisa ukusesha, imibuzo, amafayela, izimpendulo zokuskrina namaphepha agciniwe. Usinika imvume yokukugcina nokukucubungula ukuze sikusebenzisele i-Openscholar, kuhlanganise nokukuthumela kubahlinzeki be-AI abenza umsebenzi owucelayo. Layisha kuphela lokho onelungelo lokukusebenzisa.

Uma ushicilela noma wabelana ngokuthile, abantu owabelana nabo bangakubona. Uma uqhuba i-Evidence Community, unesibopho salokho ekushicilelayo.

# **I-AI nemiphumela yocwaningo**

I-AI ingenza amaphutha. Izifinyezo, izimpendulo, izinqumo zokuskrina nedatha ekhishiwe kungase kungaphelele noma kungalungile, futhi akusona iseluleko sobungcweti, sezokwelapha noma sezomthetho. Hlola imiphumela emaphepheni okuqala ngaphambi kokuthembela kuyo.

Amarekhodi amaphepha avela kubantu besithathu, kuhlanganise ne-OpenAlex. Asiwona abanikazi balawo maphepha futhi asiqinisekisi ukuthi imininingwane yawo inembile, futhi amalayisense awo ne-copyright kuyaqhubeka kusebenza.

# **Amakhredithi nezinkokhelo**

* Izici ze-AI zisebenzisa amakhredithi. Izinhlelo zamahhala ziza nesabelo senyanga, esiboniswa ekhasini lakho elithi Uhlelo namakhredithi.
* Amakhredithi awanalo inani lemali futhi awakwazi ukudluliselwa.
* Amakhredithi owathengayo, nentengo yawo, kuboniswa ngaphambi kokuthi ukhokhe. Izinkokhelo ziphathwa yi-Stripe.
* Akukho kule migomo okususa amalungelo akho asemthethweni njengomthengi, kuhlanganise nanoma yiliphi ilungelo lokubuyiselwa imali.

Singashintsha izinhlelo, amanani nezabelo. Sizokwazisa ngaphambi kokuthi ushintsho luthinte into osuyikhokhele.

# **Insizakalo yethu**

I-Openscholar inikezwa "njengoba injalo" futhi "njengoba itholakala". Asithembisi ukuthi izohlala itholakala, ingenawo amaphutha, noma ifanele inhloso yakho.

# **Isikweletu**

Akukho kule migomo okukhawulela isikweletu umthetho ongavumeli ukuthi sisikhawulele, njengokufa noma ukulimala komuntu okubangelwe ukunganaki, noma ukukhwabanisa.

Ngaphandle kwalokho, asinasibopho sokulahlekelwa okungaqondile noma okulandelayo, noma idatha, inzuzo noma amathuba alahlekile. Isikweletu sethu sisonke kuwe esihlobene ne-Openscholar sikhawulelwe kokukhulu phakathi kwalokho osikhokhele kona ezinyangeni eziyi-12 ngaphambi kwesimangalo kanye no-£100.
