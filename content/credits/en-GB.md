# Credits

OpenScholar uses work that other people have made and shared. This page names it.

## Citation styles

The citation styles and their translations come from the [Citation Style Language project](https://citationstyles.org/), from its [styles](https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles) and [locales](https://github.com/citation-style-language/locales) repositories. They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Each style and locale has its own authors. [The full list names them](/citation-styles/credits.txt).

OpenScholar converts the files from XML to JSON. When it lists references, it removes the citation number from styles that number their entries. The changed copies are offered under the same licence.

## Icons

The app's icons are from [Solar](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736) by 480 Design, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). OpenScholar recolours them to its own palette.

## Research data

The research records you search come from [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org).

- The typeface is [Ubuntu](https://design.ubuntu.com/font), © Canonical Ltd, under the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0.
- A few icons that Solar lacks are from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC licence), and the icon picker for library menus uses [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com) (MIT licence).
- Country flags are served by [Flagpedia](https://flagpedia.net) and are in the public domain.
