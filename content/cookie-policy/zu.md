# Ama-cookie

## Indlela i-Openscholar.ai esebenzisa ngayo ama-cookie

Uma usesha ku-openscholar.ai, amafayela amancane edatha abizwa ngokuthi ama-cookie angagcinwa kudivayisi yakho. Lawa mafayela asiza amawebhusayithi asebenze kahle futhi anikeze abanikazi bawo ulwazi lokuthi amawebhusayithi abo asetshenziswa kanjani. Ngezansi, sichaza ukuthi yimaphi ama-cookie i-openscholar.ai esebenzisa nokuthi asetshenziselwani.

## Ama-cookie adingekayo ngokuphelele

| **Igama** | **Inhloso** | **Iphela** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `__session`, `__client_uat`, `__client` | Akugcina ungene ngemvume | Kufika onyakeni o-1 |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Ikhumbula ulimi lwakho | Unyaka o-1 |
| `__cf_bm`, `_cfuvid` | I-Cloudflare iwasebenzisela ukuvikela iwebhusayithi ekuxhumaneni okuzenzakalelayo | Imizuzu engu-30, noma uze uvale isiphequluli sakho |

Ukukhetha kwakho kwama-cookie nezilungiselelo zokubonisa, njengamaphaneli avuliwe, kuhlala kusitoreji sendawo sesiphequluli sakho.

## Ama-cookie okusebenza nokuhlaziya (akukhethwa)

| **Igama** | **Inhloso** | **Iphela** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ph_phc_[token]_posthog`, `ph_phc_[token]_posthog_cpm` | Sisebenzisa i-PostHog ukuze sifunde ukuthi abantu bayisebenzisa kanjani i-Openscholar nokulungisa izinkinga. Lawa ma-cookie aqukethe i-ID yedivayisi engahleliwe, imininingwane yeseshini yakho nekhasi ofike kulo kuqala. Uma ungena ngemvume, aqukethe i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho, futhi i-PostHog ixhumanisa okwenzayo negama lakho nekheli lakho le-imeyili. Asilokothi sidayise ulwazi lwakho. | Izinsuku ezingu-365 |
| `_ga`, `_ga_[id]` | Sisebenzisa i-Google Analytics ukuze sibone ukuthi bangaki abantu abasivakashelayo nokuthi bafika kanjani. Lawa ma-cookie aqukethe i-ID engahleliwe nemininingwane yeseshini yakho. | Izinsuku ezingu-400 |

### Yiluphi ulwazi oluqukethwe ku-cookie ye-PostHog?

I-cookie ye-PostHog igcina imininingwane elandelayo ngefomethi ehlelekile:

- **$device_id**: Ikhodi engahleliwe nehlukile (UUID) enikezwe idivayisi yakho
- **distinct_id**: Isihlonzi esingabizwa ngegama ngenjongo yokulandela. Ekuqaleni sifana ne-device_id yakho, kodwa singaxhumana ne-akhawunti yakho uma ungena ngemvume
- **$sesid**: Imininingwane yeseshini yakho yokuphequlula yamanje, kubandakanya ukuthi iqale nini, isihlonzi seseshini esihlukile, nezikhathi zomsebenzi wakamuva
- **$epp**: Izimpawu zobuchwepheshe ezisetshenziswa yi-PostHog ngezici zangaphakathi
- **$initial_person_info**: Amarekhodi akhombisa ukuthi ufike kanjani esayithini lethu (njengokusuka kwinjini yokusesha noma ukuvakashela ngqo) nokuthi ikhasi lokuqala owafika kulo yiliphi

Le cookie ayiqukethe igama lakho, ikheli lakho le-imeyili noma eminye imininingwane eyimfihlo.

### Idatha eyengeziwe eqoqwa yi-PostHog

Ngaphandle kwe-cookie uqobo, i-PostHog iqoqa ulwazi olwengeziwe ngokuzenzakalelayo:

**Imininingwane yobuchwepheshe ngedivayisi yakho nesiphequluli sakho:**
- Isiphequluli osisebenzisayo nenombolo yenguqulo yaso
- Uhlelo lokusebenza lwedivayisi yakho
- Ukuthi usebenzisa i-desktop, ifoni yeselula, noma i-tablet
- Ukucaca kwesikrini sakho
- Umugqa wokuhlonza isiphequluli wobuchwepheshe (user agent)

**Ulwazi lwendawo olucishe lube khona:**
- Ikheli lakho le-IP (lisetshenziselwa ukulinganisa indawo kuphela, aligcinwa ku-cookie)
- Indawo ejwayelekile njengezwe, idolobha, noma isifunda (ibalwa ngekheli le-IP)
- Isilungiselelo sesikhathi sendawo yakho

**Indlela ozulazula ngayo ewebhusayithini yethu:**
- Yimaphi amakhasi obona ngesikhathi sokuvakasha kwakho
- Iwebhusayithi owavela kuyo (referrer)
- Izakhi ozichofozayo, njengezinkinobho noma amaxhumanisi
- Isikhathi osichitha ufunda ikhasi ngalinye
- Ukuthi uskrola kude kangakanani ekhasini

**Ulwazi esilukhipha ngamabomu:**
- Umbhalo owuthayipha ezimweni, ngaphandle kwalokho okufunayo
- Izitifiketi zokungena noma amaphasiwedi
- Imininingwane yokukhokha noma ikhadi lesikweletu
- Noma iyiphi eminye imininingwane yomuntu siqu eyimfihlo noma ebucayi
- Ama-cookie okukhangisa abahlinzeki bangaphandle awasetshenziswa
- Idatha yakho ayidayiswa kubakhangisi noma izinkampani zokumaketha

### Google Analytics

Uma wamukela, i-Google Analytics ithola amakhasi owavakashelayo, indlela ofike ngayo kithi, uhlobo lwedivayisi yakho nendawo yakho ecishe ibe yiyo. Ayitholi lokho okufunayo, futhi izici zayo zokukhangisa zicishiwe. Uma ushintsha umqondo, siyayimisa futhi sisuse ama-cookie ayo.

### Uma ungamukeli

Ngaphambi kokuthi ukhethe, noma uma wenqaba ama-cookie okuhlaziya, sibala kuphela ukuvakasha, ngaphandle kwama-cookie futhi singagcini lutho edivayisini yakho ngaphandle kokukhetha kwakho: ikhasi, iwebhusayithi ovela kuyo, uhlobo lwesiphequluli sakho, nezenzo ezifana nokusesha. Asirekhodi ukuchofoza kwakho, lokho okubhalayo, indawo yakho noma i-akhawunti yakho. Ikheli lakho le-IP nohlobo lwesiphequluli kuba yikhodi eshintsha nsuku zonke, futhi asiligcini ikheli lakho le-IP.

## Ukuphatha izintandokazi zakho zama-cookie

Ungabuyekeza izintandokazi zakho zama-cookie noma nini ngokuchofoza ixhumanisi elithi "Izintandokazi zama-cookie" elitholakala esiphethweni sekhasi ngalinye ku-openscholar.ai.

Ngaphezu kwalokho, zonke izisakazi-webhu ezinkulu zinezilungiselelo zokulawula ama-cookie. Ulwazi oluphelele mayelana nama-cookie nokuthi ungabona kanjani ayi-cookie agcinwe kudivayisi yakho, vakashela [www.aboutcookies.org](http://www.aboutcookies.org/) noma [www.allaboutcookies.org](http://www.allaboutcookies.org/).

### Izinhlahla zokulawula ama-cookie ngesiphequluli ngasinye:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-10-microsoft-edge-and-privacy)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
- [Microsoft Internet Explorer](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/help/17442/windows-internet-explorer-delete-manage-cookies)
- [Opera](https://www.opera.com/help/tutorials/security/privacy/)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/safari)

Kwezinye izisakazi-webhu, hlola imibhalo yosekelo enikezwe umthuthukisi wesiphequluli sakho.

### Ukususa i-cookie ye-Openscholar.ai kudivayisi yakho

Ungasusa i-cookie yethu yokuhlaziya noma nini ngokusebenzisa izilungiselelo zesiphequluli sakho:

**Google Chrome:**
1. Khetha imenyu yamachashaza amathathu (⋮) ngakwesokudla okuphezulu
2. Ya ku-Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
3. Khetha "See all cookies and site data"
4. Bhala "openscholar.ai" ebhokisini lokusesha
5. Chofoza isithonjana sokususa eduze kwe-cookie ye-PostHog

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Vula imenyu (≡) bese ukhetha Settings
2. Ya ku-Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
3. Khetha "Manage Data"
4. Bhala "openscholar.ai" ukuze uthole i-cookie
5. Yigqamise bese ukhetha "Remove Selected"

**Apple Safari:**
1. Vula i-Safari → Preferences → Privacy
2. Khetha "Manage Website Data"
3. Bhala "openscholar.ai" ensimini yokusesha
4. Khetha okufakiwe bese ukhetha "Remove"

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Chofoza imenyu yamachashaza amathathu (⋯) ngakwesokudla okuphezulu
2. Iya ku-Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
3. Khetha "See all cookies and site data"
4. Sesha "openscholar.ai"
5. Chofoza isithonjana sokususa

### Ukuvimbela ama-cookie okuhlaziya ekusetshenzisweni

Uma ungathanda ukuvimbela ukudalwa kwe-cookie yethu yokuhlaziya:

1. **Vala wonke ama-cookie**: Lungisa isiphequluli sakho ukuze siwenqabe wonke ama-cookie (qaphela: lokhu kungathinta ukuthi amanye amawebhusayithi asebenza kanjani)
2. **Vimbela ama-cookie abahlinzeki bangaphandle kuphela**: Lokhu kumisa abalandeli bangaphandle kuyilapho kuvumela i-cookie yethu yokuhlaziya yokuqala. Lokhu akuyikuvimbela i-cookie ye-openscholar.ai
3. **Faka izandiso zesiphequluli zobumfihlo**: Amathuluzi agxile ebumfihlweni njenge-Privacy Badger noma i-uBlock Origin angavimbela ama-cookie okuhlaziya
4. **Vula i-Do Not Track**: Nakuba i-openscholar.ai ingakahloniphi izimpawu ze-DNT, ungavula le nketho ezilungiselelweni zesiphequluli sakho
5. **Sebenzisa izilungiselelo zethu zama-cookie**: Chofoza "Izintandokazi zama-cookie" esiphethweni sanoma yiliphi ikhasi le-openscholar.ai ukuze ulungise izintandokazi zakho
