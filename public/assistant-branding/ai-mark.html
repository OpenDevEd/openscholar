A mark for AI, not sparkles.

Open layers round a four-point star, for every feature that uses AI. It plays once when it appears, again on hover, and loops while AI works.

Four ways in

Each one plays its load, then its hover, on repeat. Hover a mark to play its hover yourself. Orbit is the default; pick another to try it in the product below.

While it works

An endless loop for as long as AI is busy, in place of the spinner. Stopping lets the current cycle finish so the mark comes to rest instead of snapping back.
